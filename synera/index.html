Synera

Synera is a health-tracking app built for POTS and chronic illness management, combining real-time heart rate monitoring, symptom logging, and personalized alerts—all without a paywall.

Beta v1.0.11 July 4, 2025

Synera Health – Beta v1.0.11

🚀 New Features

Monitor Tab

Capability Description
Live HR Sync Zero-latency heart-rate streaming from Apple Watch with auto-sync of min / max / avg / Δ values.
Background Monitoring Passive HR capture with automatic write-back to Apple Health & background alerts.
Active Polar Sessions One-tap start of high-resolution sessions via Polar armband; auto-re-connect to last device.
Orthostatic Testing Configurable 2–3-phase tests (lie / sit / stand) from Watch or iPhone with per-phase timers.
Session History View full history of monitoring sessions & orthostatic tests (also surfaces immediately post-test).

Log Tab

  • Daily Health Log – Track overall rating, symptoms, mood, energy, sodium, fluid, events, interventions & free-text notes.
  • Card Customization – Re-order, enable / disable any log card.
  • Timeline View – Smooth month-at-a-glance timeline of all logged data.

Analytics

  • Apple Health Deep-Linking – Pull-in chronic-health-relevant metrics automatically.
  • Heart-Rate Charts – High-resolution plots with min / max / avg & HRV overlays.
  • Energy Pacing – Auto-calibrated "energy budget" points with live depletion tracking & alert hooks.
  • Activity Curves – Intraday steps curve plus 14-day comparative chart for any activity metric.
  • Wellness Trends – Mood, energy & daily-rating averages with smoothing.
  • Symptom Analytics – Heat-map plus single-symptom line chart.
  • Orthostatic Insights – Postural HR-Δ chart (lie→sit, sit→stand, lie→stand) & severity auto-grading (POTS).
  • Weather Correlation – Pull future weather for eventual cross-analysis.
  • Menstrual Phase Analysis – Flow & symptom-score charts across cycle phases.
  • Session/Test History – Unified list of all monitoring & orthostatic records.

Alerts View

  • Active / Recent Alerts – Inspect, edit, or silence.
  • Alert Types
    • Crossing Alert: HR above / below threshold (one-shot or continuous).
    • Delta Alert: HR-Δ crosses threshold.
    • Pacing Alert: Energy points below threshold (requires active Polar).
  • Global Settings – Enable/disable alerts, choose chart overlays, tune background behavior.

Settings Tab

  • Alert Manager, Notifications Scheduler, Polar Device Prefs.
  • Permissions Center – HealthKit, Bluetooth, Notifications, Voice, Location.
  • Units – Imperial / Metric for interventions & weather.
  • Info Sheets – On-demand onboarding per tab.
  • Feedback & Social Links.

Onboarding

  • App Onboarding – Full-screen, gradient walkthrough; requests Health, Bluetooth, Notification access.
  • Pacing Calibration – Dedicated sheet.
  • Tab-Specific Guides – Quick-access info sheets everywhere.

🛠 Improvements

  • Automatic Polar Re-Pairing – Faster reconnects between sessions.
  • Smoother Timeline Scrolling – Optimized rendering pipeline for large data sets.

🗒 Known Limitations (Beta)

  • Delta-Below Alerts can feel counter-intuitive; tuning in progress.
  • Apple-Watch-Only Pacing edge cases still under investigation.
  • Weather-to-Symptom Correlation Engine not yet wired into Analytics UI.
  • Menstrual Cycle Validation pending larger data set.
